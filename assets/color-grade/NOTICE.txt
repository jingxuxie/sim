Blender AgX display transform

These display LUTs and GLSL were generated with OpenColorIO 2.3.2 from Blender
4.2.8's supplied color configuration. The transform is Linear Rec.709 to sRGB,
AgX, AgX - Medium High Contrast, with -0.15 EV exposure. The included manifest
records source/configuration hashes and the individual generated asset hashes.
The GLSL implements the source LUTs' tetrahedral interpolation explicitly.
